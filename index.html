Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common seller questions about printing & sorting.

Is EvoriaBloom completely free to use? ▼

Yes! EvoriaBloom is 100% free with no monthly subscription, no watermark, and no limits on the number of labels you can crop or sort.

Are my customer details and order addresses safe? ▼

Absolutely. EvoriaBloom operates 100% inside your web browser. Your PDFs are processed in local memory using WebAssembly & JavaScript. No PDF or customer data is ever sent to any external server.

Which thermal printers are compatible with the cropped PDF? ▼

The cropped PDF output is formatted to standard 4x6 inch (or 4x4 inch) dimensions, compatible with all thermal barcode printers including TVS, TSC, Zebra, Rollo, Xprinter, Citizen, and Dymo.
